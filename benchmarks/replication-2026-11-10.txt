# cargo run --release -p squad --example replication_bench
# 4 vCPU, 15 GiB RAM. Writer, replicas and the object store (a local
# directory, file://) on the same machine and disk; RemoteSync::Sync.
# Other jobs (test builds) were active on the machine during the run.
# "lag" is the time from the writer's commit returning until the row is
# visible on a replica that polls at the given interval (300 commits each).

replica: opened from a snapshot of 220000 rows in 271.69ms
lag with a 1 ms poll interval: p50 12.94ms, p99 36.89ms, max 47.35ms
lag with a 10 ms poll interval: p50 22.38ms, p99 36.54ms, max 61.71ms
lag with a 100 ms poll interval: p50 113.57ms, p99 133.32ms, max 147.27ms
promotion until the first commit of the new writer: 103.67ms
